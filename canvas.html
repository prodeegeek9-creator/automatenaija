        FAQ

        Before you start.

        Anything else, ask June. She knows the prices, the tracks and the Arena.

        Is the community free?

        Yes. The community layer is completely free to join. You only pay for a course: Learn, a one-time ₦10,000, or the Automate build track.

        Do I need coding experience?

        No. Learn starts from zero: 13 weeks that take you from your first automation to a capstone for a real business. Automate has a Beginner level too, plus Intermediate and Expert levels for people who already know a tool like n8n or Make.

        What tools will I work with?

        n8n, WhatsApp APIs, OpenAI, Supabase, Make and related automation integrations, taught through real builds rather than isolated tutorials.

        Can I find paid work here?

        We don't run a job board, so we won't promise you postings. What you build here is the proof that wins work: every graded build goes on your public portfolio, and each one can become a verified credential that a client or employer checks for themselves, with your score and the real work behind it. The community is where builders find collaborators.

        How does the first-100 discount work?

        It's a limited-time discount, open right now to the first 100 subscribers on the Automate track. Beginner is ₦5,000 instead of ₦8,000, Intermediate ₦8,000 instead of ₦12,000 and Expert ₦10,000 instead of ₦20,000. Once the 100 spots are taken, prices return to normal.

        What's in the Learn track?

        Learn AI Automation is open now: 13 weeks covering Zapier, Make and n8n one tool at a time, then APIs, payments, AI and building for clients, ending in a capstone for a real business. Every class has notes, resources and a practice task, with live classes alongside your cohort. It's ₦10,000, paid once, and the course is yours to keep. Sign up and you can see the whole outline before you pay. Join mid-cohort and the weeks it has already covered open straight away, so you can catch up.
