FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is the community free?

Yes. The community layer is completely free to join. Paid options only unlock the structured Learn syllabus or the Automate build track.

Do I need coding experience?

No. Learn is designed for complete beginners; Automate is for people who already know a tool (like n8n or Make) and are ready to build.

What tools will I work with?

n8n, WhatsApp APIs, OpenAI, Supabase, Make and related automation integrations, taught through real builds rather than isolated tutorials.

Can I find paid work here?

Yes. The Opportunities board connects capable builders with freelance projects, hiring roles and collaborations from real businesses.
