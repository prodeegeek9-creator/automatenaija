Partner with us

Work with builders who ship, not students who watch.

Businesses, tool vendors and communities partner with Automate Naija to reach automation builders in Nigeria — and to get real problems solved while they are at it.

Ways to work together

Pick the one that matches what you need. Most partnerships end up being a mix.

Sponsor a challenge

Put your problem in the Arena

Bring a real business problem and we turn it into a build challenge. Dozens of builders attack it under a deadline, and you keep the solutions plus a shortlist of the people who built them.

Hire builders

Post to the opportunities board

Freelance projects, contract work and full roles go in front of builders whose finished work you can inspect before you talk to them.

Tools & platforms

Get your product into real builds

Credits, workshops and integration tracks that put your tool in the hands of builders who will use it on client work, not just in a demo.

Communities & schools

Run a cohort together

Bring your members through a build cycle with our tutors, our challenges and your branding on the cohort.

What partners get

  • Access to builders who have shipped something we can point at, with the work attached.
  • A named contact on our side for the length of the partnership.
  • Placement in the community, the challenge brief and the opportunities board, depending on the format.
  • A short write-up of what was built at the end, which you are free to publish.

What we ask

  • A real problem or a real role. We do not run challenges that are only marketing.
  • Clear scope, budget and timeline before anything goes live to builders.
  • Builders keep credit for what they build, and paid work gets paid on time.

Tell us what you have in mind

A sentence about your company and what you want built or hired for is enough to start.
